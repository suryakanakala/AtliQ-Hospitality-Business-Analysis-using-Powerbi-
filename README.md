## 🏨 Hospitality Business Knowledge

An important part of this project was developing a foundational understanding of how the hotel business operates.

Key concepts include:

- **Occupancy Rate** — Percentage of available rooms that are occupied over a defined period.
- **ADR (Average Daily Rate)** — Average room revenue earned per room sold.
- **RevPAR (Revenue per Available Room)** — Room revenue divided by available room nights.
- **Booking** — A reservation made for a future hotel stay.
- **Cancellation Rate** — Percentage of bookings that are cancelled.
- **Booking Channels** — Sources through which customers make reservations, such as direct hotel websites or online travel agencies.
- **Revenue Management** — Adjusting pricing and room availability to improve revenue based on demand and other business factors.

These concepts help connect dashboard metrics to actual hotel business decisions.

## 🔄 End-to-End Data Analytics Workflow

One of the key learnings from this project was understanding how a data analytics project can be executed in an industry-style workflow.

### 1. Requirement Gathering

Understand the business problem, stakeholder expectations, and decisions the analysis should support.

### 2. Mockups and Solution Design

Plan the dashboard layout, identify important KPIs, and decide how information should be presented before building the final report.

### 3. Data Collection and Understanding

Inspect the available datasets, understand their structure, identify important fields, and connect the data to the business requirements.

### 4. Data Cleaning and Transformation

Prepare the data by checking data types, missing values, inconsistencies, and other data quality issues.

### 5. Data Modeling

Organize the data and establish appropriate relationships to support consistent analysis.

### 6. Dashboard Development

Build visualizations that communicate business performance clearly and allow users to explore relevant metrics.

### 7. Insights Generation

Interpret the results, investigate performance patterns, and identify opportunities for further business analysis.

### 8. Business Recommendations

Translate validated findings into practical recommendations that hotel stakeholders can evaluate.
